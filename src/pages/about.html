About Nexora

We're a team of engineers and designers on a mission to make building software simple, fast, and enjoyable.

Our Story

Nexora started in 2020 as a small side project between three university friends who wanted to make web development less painful. Today, we help thousands of teams around the world build and ship products faster, without sacrificing quality.

Our platform is built by developers, for developers — with a strong focus on performance, security, and simplicity.
